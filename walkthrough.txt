HOW TO USE THIS WALKTHROUGH
---------------------------
Type every line that starts with ">" exactly as shown and press RETURN.
The number in [brackets] is your score after that command.
Points are shown as (+10).

 * Do NOT type HINT anywhere. Every hint costs 3 points, and you
   need all of them for the full 1070.
 * The star fragments (*) are worth 10 points each. They only turn
   up if you SEARCH the right thing in the right room.
 * Type SAVE now and then. If you die you can RETRY, or LOAD your
   saved game.
 * If the text stops with MORE, press SPACE.

=====================================================================
CHOICES THAT MATTER
=====================================================================
Some things you do (or don't do) early on change what happens later.
You can always finish the game either way.

  Chapter 3   GIVE NAPKIN TO JANITOR  -> Chapter 12: Roger gets you
              (after the burger)         the guard's keycard.
                                         Otherwise: his bucket.
  Chapter 3   KICK ASTRO CHICKEN      -> Chapter 11: the guards are
                                         fans and give you a badge.
                                         Otherwise: KICK ARCADE.
  Chapter 4   UNTIE CREWMAN           -> Chapter 13: Ricky brings a
                                         plasma spanner.
                                         Otherwise: the toolbox.
  Chapter 4   SAY GRANDMOTHER         -> Chapter 13: the pirates
              (LeChuckle's rematch)      refuse to help Vex.
                                         Otherwise: SAY MONKEY over
                                         the radio.
  Chapter 5   TAKE MONKEY             -> Chapter 11: a secret
                                         holodeck room (+10).
  Chapter 6   WEAR GLASSES            -> Chapter 11: a disguise
                                         at the checkpoint.
  All 16 star fragments               -> Epilogue: the TRUE ENDING.

=====================================================================
BAD ENDINGS - EVERY WAY TO DIE
=====================================================================
These commands end your game. After a death you can type RETRY to
start the current part again, or LOAD your saved game.

Words that mean the same thing count too:
  TAKE  = GET, GRAB, PICK         KILL  = ATTACK, HIT, FIGHT
  SHOUT = SAY, YELL, TYPE         GO    = CROSS
