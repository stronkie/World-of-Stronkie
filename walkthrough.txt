HOW TO USE THIS WALKTHROUGH
---------------------------
Type every line that starts with ">" exactly as shown and press RETURN.
The number in [brackets] is your score after that command.
Points are shown as (+10).

 * Do NOT type HINT anywhere. Every hint costs 3 points, and you
   need all of them for the full 730.
 * The star fragments (*) are worth 10 points each. They only turn
   up if you SEARCH the right thing in the right room.
 * Type SAVE now and then. If you die you can RETRY, or LOAD your
   saved game.
 * If the text stops with MORE, press SPACE.

Points per part:

=====================================================================
BAD ENDINGS - EVERY WAY TO DIE
=====================================================================
These commands end your game. After a death you can type RETRY to
start the current part again, or LOAD your saved game.

Words that mean the same thing count too:
  TAKE  = GET, GRAB, PICK         KILL  = ATTACK, HIT, FIGHT
  SHOUT = SAY, YELL, TYPE         GO    = CROSS
